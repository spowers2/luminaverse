# App Preview Video Script

**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Target Audience:** Christians seeking daily spiritual guidance

---

## Storyboard & Narration

### Scene 1 (0-5s): Daily Verse

**Visual:** Open app → Beautiful daily verse displayed with elegant typography  
**Text Overlay:** "Start Each Day Inspired"  
**Action:** Show verse changing with smooth animation, theme color visible

### Scene 2 (6-10s): Save Favorites

**Visual:** Tap heart icon to save verse → confirmation animation  
**Text Overlay:** "Save Your Favorite Verses"  
**Action:** Show favorites screen with 3-4 saved verses scrolling

### Scene 3 (11-17s): Full Bible Reader

**Visual:** Tap Bible tab → search "love" → results highlight → tap to read  
**Text Overlay:** "Search the Entire Bible"  
**Action:** Demonstrate verse search → show highlighted results → smooth scroll to verse

### Scene 4 (18-23s): Customize Experience

**Visual:** Settings screen → change theme color from Purple to Ocean Blue → toggle music  
**Text Overlay:** "Personalize Your Experience"  
**Action:** Show theme changing across screens, music toggle animation

### Scene 5 (24-30s): Streak & Close

**Visual:** Show 7-day streak badge → daily verse with meditative music playing  
**Text Overlay:** "Build a Daily Habit"  
**Final Frame:** App logo + "100% FREE • NO ADS • OFFLINE READING"

## Technical Specifications

- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (portrait, iPhone format)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 or 1290x2796 (iPhone 14 Pro)
- **Format:** .mp4 or .mov
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Max File Size:** 500MB
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds (Apple requirement)

## Recording Tips

1. **Device:** Use iPhone with latest iOS for best quality
2. **Screen Recording:** Settings → Control Center → add Screen Recording
3. **Audio:** Optional background meditation music (low volume)
4. **Editing:** Use iMovie, CapCut, or Final Cut Pro
5. **Text Overlays:** Add in post-production with consistent font (SF Pro or similar)

---

## App Store Preview Requirements

- Upload up to 3 preview videos per device size
- Videos auto-play without sound in App Store
- First 3 seconds are critical for engagement
- Include captions/text overlays (many users watch muted)
- Show actual app functionality, not marketing fluff
- Must reflect current app version
